The shape of it — assess on the left, enforce on the right

Cisco's identity story splits cleanly into two halves. Identity Intelligence and Astrix tell you what exists and what's wrong with it. Duo, AD Defense and the agentic controls do something about it. Most of the value — and most of the deployment effort — sits in wiring one to the other.

Quick answers

Does the assessment need agents installed?No. The ISA uses API-based integrations with your identity providers, chosen specifically so there's no impact on production systems and nothing to deploy on endpoints.
How long before the assessment is meaningful?Cisco allows up to 14 days after integration for the data to stabilise. Larger identity estates take longer. Don't present findings from day two.
Are the CII checks AI-generated?No. They're defined checks with published failure criteria, compatible data sources and remediation guidance for each. The AI layer is in the behavioural analytics and prioritisation, not in deciding whether a check fails.
Where does Astrix end and CII begin?Astrix's discovery engine becomes a capability inside CII rather than a separate console. Astrix's proprietary part was always the discovery engine; the MCP Secret Wrapper it published is the open piece.
Do I have to use Cisco's agent gateway?No. Duo Agentic Identity provides the authorization layer on top of whichever gateway you already run. That's the deliberate design — it meets you where your agent infrastructure already is.
Is this real-time enforcement or just visibility?Both now, but the visibility half is more mature. Independent commentary through 2026 consistently noted Cisco's identity offering was stronger on discovery than on real-time enforcement. Astrix, Duo Agentic Identity and the Britive integration are the answer to that; verify maturity per control rather than assuming parity.
What does BloodHound add that CII doesn't?Attack path intelligence. CII tells you an account is over-privileged. BloodHound shows you the chain from that account to Domain Admin, which is what makes remediation prioritisable.
Where does Splunk fit?It's the shared data plane. Human identity events, NHI events and agent activity all land in the same place, which is what lets an analyst run one investigation rather than two.

Glossary

CII
Cisco Identity Intelligence — aggregates identity data across sources and runs posture and threat checks against it
ISA
Identity Security Assessment — the pre-built CII dashboard that consolidates findings into three focus areas
ITDR
Identity Threat Detection and Response — detecting attacks against the identity layer itself
ISPM
Identity Security Posture Management — the configuration and hygiene half of the same discipline
NHI
Non-Human Identity — API keys, service accounts, OAuth tokens, workloads and AI agents
Shadow agent
An AI agent provisioned without the security team's involvement. The reason discovery has to come before governance
MCP
Model Context Protocol — how agents reach tools and data. The protocol Cisco inspects and enforces policy on
AI BOM
AI Bill of Materials — inventory of AI software assets including MCP servers and third-party dependencies
Kerberoasting
Requesting service tickets for accounts with SPNs, then cracking them offline. A classic AD service-account attack
Pass-the-Hash
Reusing a captured password hash to authenticate without ever knowing the password
BloodHound
Graph-based tool that maps privilege escalation paths through AD. Enterprise edition is the SpecterOps commercial product
User trust level
CII's per-user scoring that threat checks attach to, so findings can be prioritised by who they affect
JIT / JEA
Just-in-time and just-enough access — credentials issued for a task and revoked when it ends
Duo Passport
Carries an established Duo session across applications so users aren't re-prompted repeatedly
Per-tool-call authz
Authorising each individual tool invocation an agent makes, rather than granting access to a whole MCP server

Where to be careful

  • Assessment is not enforcement. A clean ISA report tells you what to fix. Nothing changes until someone deprovisions the accounts, tightens the MFA policy, or revokes the tokens. Budget for the remediation, not just the assessment.
  • Fourteen days is a real constraint. If a customer expects findings in a workshop next week, integrate now. This is the single most common scheduling mistake on these engagements.
  • Astrix integration is recent. The acquisition closed June 2026. Verify which capabilities are natively inside CII today versus still delivered through the Astrix console, because that gap moves every quarter.
  • Agent controls are moving fast. The gateway list, the supported MCP surface and the policy model have all changed twice in 2026. Anything you put in a design document has a short shelf life.
  • Duo AD Defense is not a full PAM replacement. It closes the legacy MFA gap and adds AD visibility. Privileged session management, credential vaulting and rotation are still separate problems.
  • Check what the ISA covers for NHIs. The three ISA sections were built around human identity. NHI and agent findings surface in CII, but not necessarily in the same pre-built dashboard.
